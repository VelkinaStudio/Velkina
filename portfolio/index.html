Pages 04 — 08 · The editor's letter

A short letter,
before we begin.

Dear visitor — most portfolios are a parade of logos and screenshots, bolted to a hero animation that took longer to build than the work it sells. This one wants to be a quieter thing: a printed journal you can read end to end, with the pages numbered, the margins lived‑in, and every project given a little air.

We make websites, applications and small AI systems for clients who care about how a thing is built — not just what it ships. The studio is two pairs of hands. We work in weekly cycles, push code on Fridays, keep our previews open, and write in plain language so a non‑engineer can sign off.

The next pages are a tour. Skip ahead with the folio on the left, or read straight through. If you find a typo, that's a feature — please write to us about it.

Yours, in cream and ink, — the studio

Pages 09 — 13 · Principles

Four rules,
tacked above the desk.

  1. i.

    Ship on Fridays.

    If it isn't on the internet by the end of the week, it isn't real. Previews, rollbacks, demos — every link is shareable.

  2. ii.

    Tokens before pixels.

    Color, type, motion: defined as a system before any screen is signed off. Consistency is cheap when it's decided early.

  3. iii.

    Numbers, not vibes.

    Server‑side analytics, simple experiments, weekly reads. We argue with data, not opinions.

  4. iv.

    Source belongs to you.

    Code lives in your organization on day one. We use open licenses where we can. Never a hostage situation.
