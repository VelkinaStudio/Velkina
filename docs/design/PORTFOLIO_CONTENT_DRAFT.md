# Portfolio content reframe — ready to apply (next session, with the comic visual world)

De-agency-fy the site into a creative-genius DUO portfolio. Drop services-as-pricing,
the Talk/Plan/Build/Ship funnel, and "Book a call". Lead with the work + the two of
us. End human. Keep lib/content.ts's honest truth-map (real clients/products).

## New section order (replaces home/page.tsx)
1. **Hero** — comic VELKINA (Inkwell), one human hook line.
2. **Statement** — who we are, with wit. NOT "we design/build/ship for clients" (agency) → instead:
   > "We're Ömer & Baha. We make software, brands, and the occasional video game — and we
   >  build the tools we make them with. Two people, a lot of range, no slide decks."
3. **Selected work** — the 6 real pieces (keep Work.tsx + lib/content.ts), framed as
   "things we've made" not "client services". Comic-panel treatment.
4. **The two of us** — Ömer + Baha as creators (keep Operators.tsx, warm it up; add
   personality — what each loves to build). NOT "two operators on every project" (agency line).
5. **What we play with** — the AI-dev / creative-tech capability (keep AIStack.tsx) reframed
   as "the stuff we're into" not a service menu. Mention the OPEN-SOURCE tools we built
   (inkwell/altpilot/hueshift/licensescan) — the genius flex: "we don't just use tools, we make them."
6. **Say hi** — human ending. Replace Contact.tsx "Three links. No form." + "Book a call":
   > "Say hi" → WhatsApp · email · (a playlist/twitter nod). NOT a sales CTA.

## REMOVE
- `Capabilities.tsx` (the 9-services list reads as an agency menu) → fold the real skills
  into "what we play with" as personality, not a price list.
- `Process.tsx` (Talk/Plan/Build/Ship is pure agency funnel).
- Nav "SERVICES" link → "WORK / PLAY / US / HI".
- content.ts `SERVICES` pricing framing; `STUDIO.process`; CONTACT "Book a call" label → "Say hi".

## KEEP (real + honest)
- lib/content.ts PROJECTS (3 clients + 3 products), OPERATORS (real bios), CONTACT links.
- The OSS repos become a real portfolio item: "Open source" section linking inkwell etc.

## Tone shift (agency → creative duo)
- agency: "Software and brand that ships. Two operators, not a department."
- portfolio: "Two people who make things — and the tools to make them. Look around."
- Austen/Gambino/sci-fi/puzzle wit in microcopy. Playful, confident, human.
