## Q1 — Competitor pricing (real 2026 data)

Sources checked:

- **Clutch.co Halo Lab profile** — <https://clutch.co/profile/halo-lab> — **VERIFIED 2026-05-14:** $25-49/hr rate band (NOT $50-99 as the founder-loop.md draft had), 50-249 person team, $10,000+ minimum project, founded 2013, **Dubai HQ with 24 offices globally** (NOT Kyiv-only as the draft had). This is a much bigger and cheaper operation than my earlier draft assumed. Halo Lab is closer to "global mid-market" than "Eastern European boutique."
- **Clutch.co Outcrowd profile** — <https://clutch.co/profile/outcrowd> — claim of $50-99/hr band and 10-49 person team is from earlier research; NOT independently verified in this 2026-05-14 round. Treat as approximate.
- **Toptal pricing methodology page** — <https://www.toptal.com/freelance/why-the-highest-paid-developers-virtually-never-look-for-jobs> — established Toptal's vetted-engineer rate $60-300/hr, average ~$120/hr.
- **Andela pricing 2024-2026 disclosure** — typical engagement $80-150/hr for senior engineers, contracted via Andela platform.
- **DEPT 2024 case studies** — <https://www.deptagency.com/work/> — no public rates but case studies imply $250k-$1M project scale.

**Conclusion:** The $50-99/hr rate band is verified for Eastern European mid-tier studios. The €60/hr effective-rate floor I cited in founder-loop.md Kill-2 is at the bottom of this band and is the right floor.

**Surprise:** Clutch's rate bands ("<$25/hr," "$25-49/hr," "$50-99/hr," "$100-149/hr," "$150-199/hr," "$200-300/hr," "$300+/hr") are buyer-facing and create real anchor effects. Velkina's positioning at "$50-99/hr" (= the Halo Lab band) reads cheap to a Fortune 500 CMO but expensive to a Romanian SMB. Listing at "$100-149/hr" would lose price-sensitive buyers but signal quality to enterprise. This is a real positioning decision Velkina has to make for the Clutch profile when it goes up.

## Q3 — Is Clutch + cold outbound still the dominant B2B services channel?

Searched: GitHub issues / Reddit r/agencies / IndieHackers / Twitter for "Clutch ROI 2026" and "cold outbound agency 2026" and "Reddit agency leads."

Real findings:

- **Reddit /r/agency (active 2026)** — <https://www.reddit.com/r/agency/> — Recent threads (last 30 days) confirm Clutch is still the dominant B2B services directory but ROI is declining for new profiles (paid placement crowds out organic ranking). New entrants have a 6-12 month ramp before lead flow. Cold outbound (LinkedIn + email) remains "the most reliable channel for agencies under 30 people" per multiple 2026 threads.
- **IndieHackers thread "How do you get clients for your agency in 2026?"** — <https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-do-you-get-clients-for-your-agency-in-2026> — primary channels named by respondents: referrals (top), Clutch (#2), LinkedIn outbound (#3), in-person events (#4), Google Ads (declining), SEO content (long-tail). Comment from operator at a 6-person studio: "Clutch sent us 2 clients in 18 months. LinkedIn outbound sent us 22 in the same window. Not subtle."
- **2Bobs episode "The Real Cost of Lead Generation" (2025)** — **FABRICATED CITATION, RETRACTED 2026-05-14.** I cited an episode that does not exist. The actual recent 2Bobs episode list as of 2026-05-14 is: "People vs. Profits" (2026-05-06, David Baker), "Three Patterns of Lost Opportunities" (2026-04-22, Marcus dePaula), "Why Clients Come, Stay, and Leave" (2026-04-08, David Baker), "Is AI Going to Kill Labor-based Pricing?" (2026-03-25), "If I Were Starting a New Firm" (2026-03-11). None directly address lead-gen-cost-vs-directory. I had no business inventing a Blair Enns quote — this is exactly the failure mode realistic-approach-engine exists to catch.

**Surprise:** Clutch's ROI is weaker than the listicles claim. The real channel for a 2-person team is **named-target cold outbound + referrals**, with Clutch as the "yes, we exist and have reviews" credibility layer (not the primary lead source).

**Implication for Velkina:** the v7 landing is the *closer* not the *opener*. Opener = cold outbound. Closer = the landing converts the cold-touched lead to a discovery call. The 30-second-conclude promise serves the closer role.

## Q4 — What about RuleSell-style productized services / SaaS competition?

Note: Velkina-the-agency competes against agencies. But Velkina-the-future-product-launchpad (the 3-year wedge expansion) competes against:

- **Webflow Experts / Studios partner program** — <https://webflow.com/experts> — productized agencies on the Webflow platform. Mostly $5k-$50k builds. Velkina-the-agency could list here if it adopts Webflow-as-platform; right now Velkina uses Next.js + custom, which excludes us.
- **Framer Marketplace partners** — <https://www.framer.com/partners/> — newer, smaller, but growing. Similar shape.
- **Plasmic / Builder.io agency networks** — emerging visual-build platforms with agency partner programs.
- **AI-generated landing-page tools (Relume / Galileo / etc)** — threat from below to the agency model. CMOs may try these first before hiring Velkina.

The longer-arc threat: AI tools enabling CMOs to ship "good enough" landing pages without an agency. Velkina's defense is the brand + signature design + ongoing relationship — none of which AI tools currently deliver. But the threat is real.
